## 55 · Finalize Athena

*grilling · resolved · blocked by 51*

Resolved 2026-10-03. Janriz saw both forms made for real on the real site (round 1, the comments above) and took my recommendation: "Agree with Haumea". The station and the orbiters are off the site; their code and models are set aside in [`research/athena-variants/removed/`](../research/athena-variants/removed/), and `athena.py` still builds them. Evidence is in [`research/athena-variants/`](../research/athena-variants/README.md).

- **Athena is Haumea, the real ringed dwarf planet:**
  - **What she is:** an egg of crystalline ice in her true proportions (2322 × 1704 × 1026 km), spinning about her short axis with the scroll. Fine craters, a few fractures, her Dark Red Spot. Her pole is tilted as the storyboard's ring was.
  - **How she's made:** an authored `.glb` from Blender by script ([`athena.py`](../research/athena-variants/athena.py)). Her ice is hollowed round her core and cut by three half-planes through her spin axis into three wedges; `public/voyage/athena/haumea.glb` is 41k triangles, 433 KB (meshopt, WebP). Her surfaces are made in code ([`tex.py`](../research/athena-variants/tex.py)): equirectangular albedo and normal maps from 3D noise.
  - **Her size:** her long radius 1.35 of the storyboard's sphere; on a portrait screen she's drawn at 0.8 of that, ring included.
- **Her ring:** narrow, in her equator's plane, made in code: a shader band of dark ice lit by her star, brighter seen towards it, dark where she shades it, all but gone against space where unlit. It's at 1.75 of her long radius (the real one is at 1.97), so she isn't lost inside it. Its shadow falls on her ice, in her shader.
- **The cutaway:** at the scroll's opening the three wedges slide straight out from her core (0.42 of her long radius). Their cut faces are shaded in code by depth, as NASA's interior diagrams draw a body: a thin bright crust, ice that darkens and blues with depth, the rock's edge warmed by the core.
- **Her core:** hot rock: dark, with seams of heat in its own emission (Hashgraph's rock); no bloom.
- **Her light:** her own star's, a directional light with a shadow map (her moons and wedges shade her), through PBR Neutral; her ice and metal reflect the road's space (`road.env`). Every part is a closed shell drawn front faces only.
- **Her moons:** two irregular, cratered ice moons (`moons.glb`, 142 KB) on the storyboard's orbits, turned to face her.
- **The pointer's peek:** before the scroll opens her, the wedge nearest the pointer opens 6% of her radius over 1.5 s (`power2.out`), follows the pointer, and reseals over 2 s; live from her arrival to the opening, with a fine pointer not over the page's UI (the pointer layer's `pointer`).
