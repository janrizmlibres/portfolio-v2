# Edolus's quantum climax: why it doesn't feel like a cut

Reviewed 2026-09-29 for *Make every scroll a scene*. Janriz wants the warp's light travel to feel like this. Sources:
- `case-studies/edolus/motion/36-scroll-chip-to-quantum-slow` (chip → burst);
- `case-studies/edolus/motion/38-scroll-quantum-to-ui-fast` (burst → Intelligence Layer card);
- the site's own scripts in `case-studies/edolus/logs/src/game-scripts.pretty.js`;
- the handoff frames at 0.45–1.2 s of clip 38 in [edolus-quantum-climax-handoff.png](edolus-quantum-climax-handoff.png).

The climax is about 3% of the film (progress 0.545–0.57) behind a ×2.5 stretch band, so it takes more wheel than anything else.

## The seven things that make it blend

1. **An anchor that never leaves the centre.** The EDL-72 chip stays dead centre from its spec sheet through the whole burst. It shrinks, tilts and folds into the crystal core the light pours out of, so the eye never has to find a new subject.
2. **A thread across the seam.** A single gold filament appears under the chip early in clip 36, is still there at the core of the burst, and is still at the centre of the Intelligence Layer card at the end of clip 38. One light bolt spans three scenes.
   - It's the `lightShot` script: comet-like bolts with a hot white core, amber and gold glow, and a wobble along the path.
3. **The new world grows out of the old subject.** The nebula doesn't fade in on top. It is revealed from the centre outward through a noise-warped front (`Nebula`: `organicFadeIn`, `revealFromCenter`), so the burst is born from the chip.
4. **The old room smears; it doesn't fade.** A scroll-driven sweep (`cubesTransition`, the "quantum" SFX's trigger) shears the rack and floor into horizontal slabs with rainbow chromatic fringes (aberration 12). It reads as speed, not as a dissolve. This is where the light-speed feel comes from.
5. **The world becomes a picture.** At the peak, the full-screen burst closes into a rounded card, and the card runs the **same nebula shader at the same zoom** (`Nebula2`, whose zoom "matches nebula.js"). So the space you were inside becomes the picture in the frame. The particle field lines spill past the card's edges as it closes, then disperse. Then the title splits around the card ("INTELLIGENCE" above, "LAYER" below), and gold ribbons sweep across the new dark background.
6. **One colour across the seam.** Gold is the only colour from the map through to the Intelligence Layer, so the cut never changes the palette.
7. **Sound carries it.** The quantum loop's pitch rises with scroll speed (1.0–1.6×) and fades out over 0.8 s after the wave leaves. A short accent marks the card's arrival.

## What it suggests for the warp's light travel

The same moves, mapped onto *pull back → flare → get ready → light travel → arrive*:
- **Anchor:** the bot stays centred through the whole warp, and it's what the visitor rides with.
- **Thread:** a trail of light from the bot, or from the explored star it heads for, runs from departure to arrival and stays in the destination scene for a moment.
- **Grow from the subject:** the light travel is born out of the flared star, not laid over the frame.
- **Smear, don't fade:** the scene being left shears into streaks. That's the wormhole's throat.
- **Arrive as a picture:** the tunnel closes into the destination's point of light, which opens into the scene. That's the wormhole's white hole.
- **One colour across the seam:** the warp is the warmest moment on the page, since warmth is energy. It cools into the destination's own hue on arrival.
- **Sound follows speed**, as the quantum loop does.
