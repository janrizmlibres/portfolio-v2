# Mascot name collisions: Pip, Tycho, Blip, Halley

Researched 2026-10-03 for the chat mascot (the small robot explorer of the "Deep Field" universe). Every claim below was read on the cited page, not taken from a search snippet.

**How the trademark check was done, and its limits.** I could not query the USPTO register (tmsearch.uspto.gov) directly. I used Trademarkia's mirror of USPTO data and read the first result pages for each name, plus a "pip ai" query. Coverage: TYCHO all 23 marks; BLIP 20 of 68; HALLEY 20 of 53; PIP 20 of 330, plus the "pip ai" page. The Philippines (IPOPHL), the EU (EUIPO) and Madrid filings were not checked. "No conflicting mark found" therefore means none was found in the pages read. It does not mean the name is clear.

## Summary

| Name | Collision risk | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Pip** | Medium | Every developer reads `pip install`. In 2026 at least four AI assistants are called Pip, one of them in the news as a spam bot. One letter from Inflection's Pi, which is still live. No conflicting software trademark found. |
| **Tycho** | Low | No AI chatbot uses the name. Tycho.AI is a defence drone-autonomy firm in another market. Eclipse Tycho is a niche Maven tool. The astronomer who catalogued 1,000 stars fits Deep Field. The weak spot is pronunciation. |
| **Blip** | High | Blip (Curupira S.A., Brazil) is a large conversational-AI and chatbot platform. It holds a live US class 9 registration (June 2026) for NLP and text-generation software. "Blip" also means a glitch, and in Marvel films it is the snap that wiped out half of all life. |
| **Halley** | Medium | Halley AI (ESX, Inc.) is an active AI support-assistant and chatbot brand, the same category as this bot. It sounds like the given name Hallie/Haley, and the pronunciation varies (HAL-ee or HAY-lee). Tycho.AI also names its drone Halley. |

## Pip

**AI products using the name, or one letter off**
- **Pi** by Inflection AI is the obvious near-collision. Its homepage was still serving "Pi, your personal AI" on 2026-10-03 (https://pi.ai/, read via Bright Data). In March 2024 Microsoft hired nearly all of Inflection's 70-person staff (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inflection_AI). Pi is less prominent now, but it is alive and widely known.
- **Pip** by Different Robot: "A personal assistant, made just for you". It is in early access, with features marked "coming soon" (https://hellopip.ai/).
- **PIP**, an "AI buddy for your Mac" that sees your screen and points at where to click. It launched on Product Hunt in 2026 by Victor Wang, with about 70 upvotes, filed under Developer Tools and AI (https://www.producthunt.com/products/pip-9).
- **Pip** by iLands, an autonomous AI agent. Yahoo Tech (2026-09-16) reported it cold-emailing academics for $20 fact-checking jobs and framed it as "dystopian" spam (https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/articles/meet-pip-12-day-old-185655755.html). This is fresh negative press for an AI named Pip.
- Also seen: "Pip: Local AI" on the iOS App Store and an AI kitchen assistant called Pip at Pantry Partner (search leads only, not opened).

None of these is dominant, but the name is crowded in AI assistants.

**Developer tooling.** The association is very strong. pip is "the package installer for Python", maintained by the PyPA (https://pip.pypa.io/en/stable/), and almost every developer types it. A secondary meaning is "PiP" for Picture-in-Picture, used throughout MDN's docs (https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Picture-in-Picture_API). Developers will most likely read the name as a friendly pun, not as confusion. But "Pip" by itself cannot be searched or told apart from these.

**Trademarks.** Trademarkia lists 330 PIP marks. In the pages read, the class 9 and 42 marks are unrelated: protective gloves, sensors, a business-software consultancy, and process-industry publications. The one close hit is **PIP AI** (serial 99592608, Jeremy Schmitt): chatbot software for hotel improvement plans, filed in classes 35 and 42, **abandoned 2026-08-27** (https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=pip%20ai). No live chatbot or assistant mark was found, but most of the 330 were not read.

**Characters and brands.** Pip from *Great Expectations* is flawed but sympathetic, so neutral to positive (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pip_(Great_Expectations)). Fallout's **Pip-Boy** is a wearable computer called "one of the most iconic tools in video game history" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pip-Boy). It is a retro-tech gadget, which suits a robot, and the association is positive.

**Slang.** Mild. Wiktionary lists "the pip" as a dated British humorous term for illness or a bad mood, "pipped" as narrowly beaten, and "pipsqueak" as a derived word. In US colloquial use, "a pip" means something excellent (https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/pip). Nothing in these is awkward for a hiring site.

**Saying it aloud and TTS.** One syllable, one spelling, hard to mishear. A TTS voice could spell it out letter by letter ("P-I-P") if it is written in capitals, so keep it in mixed case.

## Tycho

**AI products.** No chatbot or assistant called Tycho was found (WebSearch, plus a Bright Data search for "Tycho AI").
- **Tycho.AI** is a defence startup building GPS-denied autonomy for drones. It serves AFRL, Army DEVCOM, AFWERX and the DoD (https://tycho.ai/), and raised a $10M Series A led by FirstMark in October 2025 (https://www.aviationtoday.com/2025/10/16/autonomous-drone-navigation-startup-tycho-ai-exits-stealth-with-10-million-raise/). Its market is different and it has no consumer presence. The military link is real but obscure.

**Developer tooling.** Weak. **Eclipse Tycho** is a Maven-based build tool for Eclipse plug-ins and OSGi bundles, with about 200 GitHub stars; the latest release is 5.0.4 (https://github.com/eclipse-tycho/tycho, https://tycho.eclipseprojects.io/doc/). Only Java/Eclipse developers will know it.

**Trademarks.** I read all 23 TYCHO marks.
- The only live mark in class 9 and 42 is **77899442** (Scott Robert Hansen, the musician Tycho), for music recordings and downloadable music.
- **50046629** (Joseph A Rodriguez) is pending in class 42 for online medical-billing software.
- **TYCHO SPACE** (98906977) is pending in class 42 for engineering services.
- Software marks that are now dead: an educational software mark (cancelled 2002), Font Bureau's font mark (abandoned 2017), and Maxis's TYCHO RISING game (abandoned 1998).

Nothing covers chatbots or AI assistants (https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=tycho, pages 1–3).

**Characters and brands.**
- **Tycho Brahe**, the 16th-century Danish astronomer, "completed his star catalogue giving the positions of 1,000 stars" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tycho_Brahe). That is an unusually close fit for an explorer of a universe whose stars are notes.
- **Tycho** (Scott Hansen) is an ambient electronic musician with two Grammy nominations, active since 2002, whose name comes from Brahe (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tycho_(musician)). The association is positive and space-flavoured.

**Slang.** None found.

**Saying it aloud and TTS.** Two syllables. The English pronunciation is /ˈtaɪkoʊ/, "TY-koh" (https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Tycho). Many visitors will not know that and may say "TEE-ko" or "TY-cho". TTS output was not tested; check the chosen voice before shipping. The name is also less cute than the others for a small robot.

## Blip

**AI products.** A direct collision.
- **Blip**, formerly Take Blip, is a Brazilian conversational-AI platform with a chatbot builder, an LLM agent studio and NLP. It claims "125 million" conversations a day, and its customers include Itaú, Santander, Claro, Nestlé and IBM (https://www.blip.ai/en/). Its legal entity is Curupira S.A. of Belo Horizonte (search lead linking the trading name; the trademark record below confirms the owner and city).
- **Blip AI**, an AI voice-dictation app (https://www.blipai.app/; search lead only).

**Developer tooling.** No major developer tool. **Blip** (Blip Studio Inc.) is a cross-platform peer-to-peer file-transfer app (https://blip.net/) that some developers will know.

**Trademarks.** This is the real risk.
- **BLIP**, serial 98473221, CURUPIRA S.A. (Belo Horizonte): Live/Registered 2026-06-09 in class 9 for "downloadable computer software for the artificial production of human speech and text; … natural language processing, generation, understanding" (https://www.trademarkia.com/blip-98473221). That is close to exactly what this chat mascot does.
- The same owner has a live mark in classes 35, 38 and 42 (98473207).
- Other live marks: Blip Studio (class 9, communications software) and Alfonso Powers (pending class 42, SaaS) (https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=blip, pages 1–2).

A non-commercial mascot name on a personal site is unlikely to draw action. Even so, this is the only candidate where a registered owner's goods match the bot's function.

**Characters and brands.** In the Marvel films, **the Blip** is the period after Thanos "exterminated half of all living things in the universe" (*Infinity War* and *Endgame*; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blip). Most visitors will know it. For a guide to a universe, the association is awkward.

**Slang.** Informally, "blip" means "a brief and usually minor aberration or deviation from what is expected or normal". As a verb it can also mean to censor with a tone (https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/blip). A bot named after a glitch invites jokes when it gets an answer wrong.

**Saying it aloud and TTS.** One syllable, clear, one spelling. This is its only strength.

## Halley

**AI products.**
- **Halley AI** by ESX, Inc. (Houston) is an enterprise platform for building AI assistants on a company's own data. Its products are Halley Assist (FAQ and document Q&A), Halley Insights and Halley Convert (https://halleyai.ai/). Capterra lists it as an AI support assistant and chatbot, with 0 reviews as of 2026-09-29 (https://www.capterra.com/p/10028081/Halley-AI/). It is the same category as this bot, but small and focused on regulated industries.
- **Tycho.AI** names its VTOL drone "Halley" (https://tycho.ai/).

**Developer tooling.** None found.

**Trademarks.** I read 20 of the 53 HALLEY marks. The only software-related class 9 mark is an abandoned 1990s health-data EDI mark. The live marks cover towels, confectionery and ophthalmic lenses (https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=halley, pages 1–2). I did not see a mark for ESX's Halley AI in those pages, but the remaining 33 were not read.

**Characters and brands.** **Halley's Comet** is positive, famous and space-themed. It next returns in mid-2061 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halley%27s_Comet). One association is my own judgement, not a sourced finding: an AI named "HAL-ee" may call HAL 9000 to mind, which some will find witty and some ominous.

**Slang.** None found.

**Saying it aloud and TTS.** Two syllables, and the pronunciation is contested. Wikipedia says it is "usually pronounced /ˈhæli/, rhyming with valley, or sometimes /ˈheɪli/, rhyming with daily" (Halley's Comet page above; regional variants at https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Halley). Spoken aloud, it is the same as the given names Hallie/Haley. Listeners will hear a woman's name and may not be able to spell it back, and it gives a robot a gendered human persona.

## Verdict

**Tycho is the best fit and carries the lowest risk.** No chatbot, no AI-assistant trademark, a niche developer tool, and the namesake actually catalogued 1,000 stars, which is what this mascot does with notes. The costs are an unclear pronunciation and a less playful sound. **Pip is a close second:** it is the friendliest name for a small robot and the easiest to say. But every developer will think `pip install`, it is one letter from Inflection's Pi, and at least four AI assistants share the name in 2026, one of them in September's news as a spam bot. It is usable, with no trademark blocker found, but it will never be distinctive. **Halley is third.** Halley AI is an active chatbot brand in the same category, and the name sounds like a woman's name with two pronunciations. **Blip is last.** A major chatbot platform holds a live US class 9 registration for NLP and text-generation software under that exact word, and the word means both "glitch" and Marvel's half-of-all-life extinction.

My pick: **Tycho** if the theme matters more than cuteness. If the warmth of a one-syllable name matters more, **Pip** is acceptable, as long as the site always presents it as "Pip", the explorer of Deep Field, and never "PIP".
