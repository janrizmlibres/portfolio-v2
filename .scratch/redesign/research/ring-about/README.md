# Rework About on the ring ship — evidence

- `ring.py`, `export.py`, `build.sh`: the finished ring (and its visitor), built headless in Blender, baked and compressed to `public/voyage/ship/ring.glb`. Take `.locks/heavy` before `./build.sh`.
- `layout.py`, `paths.py`: the rough layout tool used to sketch the camera's beats before the real site.
- `tools/around*.ts`: Around home's framing computed off the page (`npx tsx --tsconfig tsconfig.json …`).
- `checks.js` (drag, reduced-motion stills), `probe.js` (GPU at the settle), `measure.js` (frame cost, `SECS` for a fast scroll), `aa.js` (contrast). The shots and recordings use `../hero-and-home/shots.js` and `record.js` with `OUT` pointed here.
- `out/about-ring-{1440,390}.mp4` + sheets: the walkthroughs; `out/settle-vs-janriz.png`: the settle beside Janriz's frame.
- `shots/spots/`: every reading spot to Around home, at 1440 and 390; `shots/r1–r6`: in-betweens by round.
