# Redesign — wow factor

Labels: wayfinder:map

## Destination

The redesigned site, live. Its creative concept, visual language and **signature moments** (motion, 3D, sound, the AI entrypoint) are decided, then built and tuned on the real site, each with its tech, asset source, fallbacks and budget. The site meets the performance budget and passes the QA plan. Reached when no open tickets and no fog remain.

## Notes

- **Domain & glossary:** `CONTEXT.md`. Use its terms; update it inline via `/domain-modeling` when a term resolves.
- **Skills per ticket type:** grilling → `/grilling` + `/domain-modeling`; research → `/research` subagent; prototype → `/prototype`. GSAP / Three.js / library API facts via Context7.
- **Nothing in the design is final until a ticket settles it.** The 2026-09-27 prototype (`docs/design/2026-09-27-redesign-prototype.html`) and [prototype-decisions.md](prototype-decisions.md) are reference only, along with the review answers that follow from its layout. What has settled is in Decisions so far. **What stands** besides:
  - the facts, sections and their ids (*Pick the creative concept*): roles, dates and titles from the resume; LogoLife's title "Full-Stack / AI Engineer"; Elinnov and Yearbook stay; the availability line;
  - the NDA line, an AGENTS.md rule;
  - the effects rule below.
- **Ambition:** a full award-site build is welcome.
- **Standing constraints — every moment honours all five:**
  1. GSAP is the only animation library. Three.js (or whatever renders 3D) only draws; GSAP drives it. Smooth scroll via GSAP's ScrollSmoother, not Lenis. In the voyage itself the film's own clock on GSAP's ticker is the easing, and ScrollSmoother smooths only the plain page (ADR 0003's amendment, *Build the film engine*).
  2. Text stays real HTML — name, copy, links are never canvas-only (SEO, LLM crawlers, screen readers).
  3. One gate only: the wake-up (ADR 0005, *Plan the signature moments*, Janriz 2026-10-01). Every load opens on Tycho charging up while the first scene loads, and the visitor's click wakes him and turns the sound on. The page's HTML is complete under it from first paint. No other loader or intro gates the page.
  4. Every moment has a reduced-motion branch and a lighter mobile tier; 3D and audio load lazily after first paint.
  5. LCP ≤ 2.5 s on a mid-range phone; animation holds 60 fps on a mid-range laptop.
- **Progressive enhancement is the baseline:** the page's HTML is complete from first paint, under the wake-up, while the first scene's universe, the bot and sound load. Everything past the first scene loads after the wake. Without WebGL there is no wake-up, only the plain page.
- **Effects rule:** bespoke effects tied to the concept are welcome; generic template effects (glow, bloom, blinking cursor, pulsing dots, gradient blobs, purposeless parallax) stay banned. No game-dev concept required.
- **Chat role** (ADRs 0007 and 0008): Tycho (called Pip, the stand-in's name, until 2026-10-03) talks in his bubble, revealing replies at a reading pace, and the log holds the conversation. Everything the chat says is in Tycho's voice, not Janriz's. The agent moves the page through the tool effects in ADR 0005.
- **Assets:** free and self-made by default — code-generated sound, procedural or self-modelled 3D, CC0 assets, Google Fonts. Anything paid needs its own case and Janriz's approval.
  - **Fonts** (Janriz, 2026-09-27): a closed licence is fine if the font is free and the licence allows commercial web use ("free to download" alone is not enough); its terms bind the build. Fontshare's ITF Free Font License (e.g. General Sans): credit optional, but serve the official files unmodified (no subsetting or format conversion) and never commit them to this public repo — load them from the Fontshare API, or fetch them at build time and keep them out of git.
  - **Sound** (Janriz, 2026-09-27): free only, never paid stock libraries (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Envato and the like). Free music and SFX are welcome if their licence allows commercial web use; NC licences are excluded.
- **Case studies:** all six resolved, one folder per site at `case-studies/<site>/` ([FORMAT.md](case-studies/FORMAT.md), [BRIEF.md](case-studies/BRIEF.md)). Third-party captures stay in `.scratch/`, never the public repo. Trimmed 2026-10-10: Edolus, Trionn and US Avionix keep their full captures; Izanami, Produx and Hashgraph Ventures keep only their README and contact sheets.
  - **Every design ticket goes back to them** (Janriz, 2026-10-01). Before proposing a design (a scene, a seam, the scroll's feel, how text sits) in a prototype or grilling ticket, re-read the relevant parts of Edolus, Trionn and US Avionix (the other three are READMEs only). Name the ideas taken from each in the proposal or ticket comment, and add your own. Nothing in them is absolute.
  - **Build tickets don't read them** (*Plan the signature moments*). The vision is in the prototypes they build from (the skeleton) and in their finalization grillings (the look), which are design tickets and do read them. A full reread costs about 89K tokens. The one exception: where the shot list names a case study as a scene's source (Trionn's ribbon, Hashgraph's object opening towards the pointer, Edolus's quantum climax), a build ticket may open that one study's section for its numbers.
- **Research findings** live at `research/<slug>.md` here (the tracker is untracked, so no `research/*` branches).
- **Capture & recording:** MP4 via scripted headless Playwright (`research/capture/capture.js`), at most 2 capture runs at once on this machine. Claude in Chrome can't record (a hidden tab never animates). Janriz can't be assumed present on AFK tickets.
- **This map carries execution** (Janriz, 2026-09-27), overriding wayfinder's "plan, don't do" and the `/to-spec` → `/to-tickets` hand-off: feel can only be judged on the real site.
  - **The prototypes are the skeleton, never the final look** (Janriz, 2026-10-02: "the prototype is NEVER the final look. it's the skeleton").
    - **What a build keeps from a prototype:** its camera path and timings, its composition, its text and manners, and the type.
    - **Every body, surface and element in a prototype is a stand-in,** however finished it was called. *Finish one scene* called home finished, and *Build the hero and home* ported it as a placeholder.
    - **Each build ticket with a look of its own is blocked by a finalization grilling** (*Finalize …*). It settles what each body is, how it's made (ADR 0002's routes), and its materials and light.
    - **Janriz picks from variants made for real,** shown in place on the real site, never described.
    - **No finalization:** *Build the audio engine* (groundwork) and *Rewrite the chat in Tycho's voice* (copy, which *Name and voice the mascot* settles).
  - **Build tickets** are `task` tickets sized to one session and blocked by the decisions they need, their finalization among them. AFK by default; HITL when the result needs Janriz's eye or ear. Test-first where there's logic (Red → Green → Refactor, with a named Refactor verdict); pure visual or sound tuning is checked by a recording or a listening pass instead.
  - **Gate:** no build ticket for UI or a signature moment starts until *Pick the creative concept*, *Pick the AI entrypoint*, *Prototype the visual language*, *Make every scroll a scene*, *Storyboard the voyage* and *Finish one scene* resolve. Groundwork that doesn't depend on the look (the 3D loader, the audio engine, ScrollSmoother, *Stop sending chunks to the browser*, *Export the sky*) starts once its own verdict resolves.
  - **Durable record instead of a spec:** each verdict writes its ADR in `docs/adr/`; terms go into `CONTEXT.md` as they settle; Decisions so far is the index.
  - **Every build ticket** follows the AGENTS.md SEO/GEO sync rows its content triggers and keeps the standing constraints. Code comments, commits and PR text state their rule inline and cite nothing outside the public checkout — `CONTEXT.md`, `docs/adr/` and this tracker are local-only here (`.git/info/exclude`).
  - **Branch and PR:** the portfolio can't half-ship, so the redesign builds on a branch. The PR (or a stack) comes after the last ticket and is never a ticket itself.
